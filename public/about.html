About DJ Wolverine

Written by DJ Wolverine • Oct 12, 2024 • Nairobi, Kenya • Updated Sep 4, 2026

With over a decade behind the decks, DJ Wolverine is the resident selector for Overkill Entertainment — crafting unforgettable atmospheres for club nights, festival mainstages, luxury weddings and high-stakes corporate galas. He doesn't just play music; he reads the room and becomes part of the energy.

From deep house and Amapiano to Afro-tech and 90s RnB, every transition is harmonic, every build is intentional. Based in Nairobi, available across East Africa and beyond.
